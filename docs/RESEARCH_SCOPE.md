# Research claims and qualification boundary

CROSS is a controlled synthetic model of the intersection of two maritime traffic streams involving autonomous and human-operated vessels. It can be used to reproduce documented simulation experiments and explore sensitivity, robustness and candidate coordination policies **within the modeled assumptions**.

It is **not** calibrated against AIS field data; it does **not** identify measured waterway capacity, replace expert navigation judgment, or certify any operational collision-avoidance system.

The qualified-default PSP reference mode and post-1.0 metered exploratory mode are **not scientifically interchangeable**. Changed physics, distributions or fleet mixtures require their own validation and evidence. Synthetic modeled near misses are not direct empirical casualty or safety rates.

Source provenance and original scientific result bytes must remain traceable to the separately archived immutable R2 and PSP payloads.
